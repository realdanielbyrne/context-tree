Caveats

  • Single local model, single synthetic task — not a published benchmark; a non-Docker SWE-bench harness is validated and is the proper next substrate.
  • turns = 61 means the 60-turn budget was exhausted, not "cannot finish".
  • Grading is all-or-nothing; FAIL cells are not distinguished by how close they came.
  • total_prompt_tokens is raw — context volume, not cached cost.
  • temp 0 but not bit-identical on this host; read differences against the min–max spreads.
  • Pooling assumes the batches are exchangeable (same code, model, task, cap, turn budget). Per-batch numbers are shown so this is auditable.
